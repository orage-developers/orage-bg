# Template: Proposal

- **File**: `templates/proposal.html`
- **Formats**: DOCX, PDF
- **Use for**: client proposals.

## Structure

1. Compact header.
2. Eyebrow: `Proposal · <client>`. H1: the outcome for the client. Lead
   line with the result and the date. Meta line: prepared for, date,
   validity.
3. `Where you are`: the client's constraint in their words and numbers.
4. `What we build`: one feature box per product (eyebrow, H3, one line).
5. `Investment`: price box (gold border) with the offer, price and trust
   line from the guidelines.
6. `Timeline`: table `Week`, `Milestone`.
7. `Next step`: one CTA line with `<span class="arrow">→</span>`.

## Content

- Uses the compact type scale: H1 24pt, H2 14pt, H3 11.5pt, body 10pt,
  price 20pt. Keep sizes consistent; do not enlarge single elements.
- Products, prices and guarantees come only from the guidelines.
