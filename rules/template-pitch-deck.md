# Template: Pitch deck

- **File**: `templates/pitch-deck.html`
- **Formats**: PPTX, PDF
- **Use for**: sales decks for Orage offers.
- **Also follow**: [format-pptx.md](format-pptx.md).

## Structure

1. Cover: positioning headline.
2. The problem: three bullets in the buyer's terms.
3. What we build: three product cards.
4. The offer: three tier cards side by side; the recommended tier has the
   gold border (`card featured`).
5. How it compounds: one idea with a supporting card.
6. Closing: CTA with an arrow and the booking link.

## Content

- For a shorter deck, keep the cover, what we build, the offer and the
  closing slide. Drop the slides that would stay placeholders first.

- Offers, prices and products only from the guidelines.
- Use the three-card grid (`grid3`) for any side-by-side comparison.
