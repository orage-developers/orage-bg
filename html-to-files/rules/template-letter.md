# Template: Letter

- **File**: `templates/letter.html`
- **Formats**: DOCX, PDF
- **Use for**: letters and formal notes to one recipient.
- **Length**: one page.

## Structure

1. Compact header.
2. Date, then recipient name, title and company.
3. Eyebrow: `Re: <subject>`.
4. Greeting, opening paragraph with the result, one supporting paragraph,
   one clear next step. Under 120 words for the body.
5. `Talk soon,`, the name in Bebas Neue, then title, email and website.
