# Template: One-pager

- **File**: `templates/one-pager.html`
- **Formats**: PDF (primary), DOCX
- **Use for**: a one-page overview of Orage or one offer.
- **Length**: exactly one page in the PDF.

## Structure

1. Compact header.
2. Eyebrow, H1 positioning line, lead line, one short paragraph.
3. `What you get`: table `Product`, `What it does`.
4. `Pick your tier`: offer ladder table `Tier`, `Offer`, `Price`, `Delivery`.
5. Recommended box: offer, price, trust line, CTA.

## Content

- Cut words before shrinking type. Check the PDF page count is 1.
