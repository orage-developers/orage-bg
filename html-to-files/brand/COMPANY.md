## What Orage does

Orage finds the highest-leverage point in a business, the one change that
produces the most compounding return, and solves it with custom AI-powered
systems built for that operation. No off-the-shelf software: everything is
designed for the client's business.

Examples from the site: an AI that handles client onboarding, a workflow that
removes manual reporting, a tool that flags churn risk before it happens, a
marketing system that runs without the team managing it daily.

## Who it is for

Service businesses doing over $1M in annual revenue: agencies, consultancies,
coaching operations and professional service firms that are growing but feel
the business is running them. Signs: the team is stretched, margins are
tightening, or clients leave faster than new ones arrive.

## How an engagement works

1. **Strategy session** (no commitment): map the operation, find the biggest
   constraint, show which lever Orage would pull and the expected outcome.
2. **Diagnostic**: workflows, team, clients and business model.
3. **Build and integrate**: custom AI systems that fit how the team works, so
   the team adopts them instead of working around them.
4. **Plan**: a clear plan with defined deliverables and expected outcomes.

The lever depends on the constraint: marketing, conversions, operations or
customer success.
